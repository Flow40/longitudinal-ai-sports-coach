# Workout Review Prompt

Review the completed session.

Compare:

- prescribed versus completed exercises
- prescribed versus actual loads
- repetitions completed
- RPE
- pain before, during and after
- technique or tolerance notes

Identify:

- what progressed
- what was poorly tolerated
- whether the next session should increase, maintain or reduce load
- whether any restriction should be added, updated or removed
- what information is worth storing in long-term memory

Do not diagnose injuries.
