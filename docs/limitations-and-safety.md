# Limitations and Safety

## Scope

This project is exploratory and based on a single-person case study.

## Main Limitations

- no controlled comparison with a human coach
- no validated clinical evaluation
- incomplete or inconsistent user reporting
- possible model hallucinations
- possible memory errors
- outdated constraints may remain active
- subjective interpretation of pain and fatigue
- limited generalizability

## Medical Safety

The system must not:

- diagnose injuries
- override medical advice
- normalize severe or unexplained pain
- encourage training through red-flag symptoms
- present uncertain conclusions as facts

The system should:

- use conservative progression
- state uncertainty
- recommend professional evaluation when needed
- distinguish training guidance from medical advice
- ask for updated status after injury or treatment

## Privacy

Sensitive data may include:

- medical history
- imaging results
- age and identity
- performance history
- travel and schedule information

Recommended safeguards:

- anonymize all public examples
- never publish raw conversations
- avoid publishing medical documents
- separate identity from training data
- retain only useful information
- allow deletion and correction of memory

## Ethical Position

The project is not intended to replace coaches, physiotherapists or physicians. Its purpose is to explore how AI can support continuity, personalization and documentation.
